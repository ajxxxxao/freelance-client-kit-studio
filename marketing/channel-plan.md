# Cold Start Channel Plan

## Objective

Collect 10 to 20 real feedback responses from freelancers, consultants, and solo service providers before investing in more product polish or paid distribution.

This plan avoids broad, low-context promotion. The first goal is learning, not scale.

## Phase 1: Personal / Manual Feedback

### Goal

Get the first 5 to 8 useful feedback responses from people who can understand the freelancer client-document workflow.

### Channels

- Direct messages to freelancers or consultants in your existing network
- Past collaborators or clients who work with service providers
- Small private groups where you already have permission to ask
- One-on-one messages to people who regularly send proposals or invoices

### What to Post/Send

Use a short personal message:

"I built a local-first tool for freelancers to create proposals, invoices, contract terms, and client emails. I am looking for honest feedback on whether the workflow is clear and useful. Would you be open to taking a quick look?"

Include:

- Landing page
- App link
- Early access feedback form

### Success Signal

- 5 to 8 people open the product
- 3 to 5 people give specific feedback
- At least 2 people can describe the value without extra explanation

### Risk

People may be polite instead of direct. Ask specific questions and invite critical feedback.

## Phase 2: Small Community Testing

### Goal

Reach 10 to 20 total feedback responses from users outside the immediate network.

### Channels

- Small freelancer communities
- Designer or developer communities with self-employed members
- Consultant-focused Slack or Discord groups
- Niche Reddit threads where feedback requests are allowed
- Indie maker communities with a feedback or show-and-tell section

### What to Post/Send

Post a concise feedback request:

- Explain who the product is for
- Say it is local-first and does not require an account
- List the document types: proposal, invoice, contract terms, email
- Ask for feedback on clarity, usefulness, and willingness to pay $9 one-time

Avoid claims about revenue, automation, or replacing professional tools.

### Success Signal

- 10 to 20 total feedback responses
- At least 3 repeated pain points or feature requests
- At least 3 people say they would use it for a real client workflow
- At least 2 people show interest in the early access list

### Risk

Communities may treat promotional posts poorly. Lead with the feedback request and be transparent that the product is early.

## Phase 3: Public Launch Preparation

### Goal

Prepare a small public launch only after the positioning, onboarding, and Pro value are validated.

### Channels

- Indie Hackers post
- Product Hunt upcoming page or small launch
- Reddit post in approved communities
- LinkedIn post from a personal profile
- Small newsletter sponsorships only after conversion signals exist

### What to Post/Send

Use a launch story:

- The problem freelancers face with client documents
- Why local-first matters
- What the product can create
- What feedback changed from early testing
- Clear CTA to try the workspace or join early access

### Success Signal

- More people click into the app than only the landing page
- Users generate at least one document during testing
- Early access signups come from people who match the target user
- Feedback confirms which Pro features are worth building

### Risk

Launching too early can attract shallow traffic but little learning. Do not launch broadly until the product has a clear first-use moment and the Pro promise is easy to understand.
